About me

From my first code to design with purpose

I am 19 years old and I study Information and Communication Technologies (ICT) Engineering, specializing in multiplatform software development.

My first real contact with code was at high school (Cobach), where I built a supplements website in HTML and CSS. I enjoyed it so much that I decided to study programming.

Since high school I have used Canva almost every day: I designed covers and magazines, and I even sold some of them. Today I combine both worlds: code to build, and design to communicate.

I am interested in web development, user interface design, editorial design and ideas related to fitness and well-being.

How I work

Clean, intentional work

I prefer clean designs without excess. Every element needs a reason, and I check spelling, alignment and consistency before delivering.

Ethics first

I give credit to sources, respect other people's work and privacy, and say clearly when a project is academic or fictional.

Open to feedback

I listen, iterate and improve. I know I will design for other people, not only in my own style.

My goals

To be an efficient programmer who adapts

My goal is to become an efficient programmer who adapts to people's needs and to new technologies, always with ethics, good frontend design and a clean, organized, high-quality backend.

I want to build software that is useful, easy to use and responsible: something that solves a real problem and works well on any device.

  • Frontend with careful, modern and accessible design
  • Backend that is ordered, secure and maintainable
  • Constant learning of new tools and technologies
  • Ethical decisions in every project
